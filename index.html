About Me

I am currently pursuing my Ph.D. in the Theory Group in the School of Computer Science at Nanjing University, advised by Prof. Penghui Yao. My research lies at the intersection of quantum computing and theoretical computer science, specifically focusing on quantum information, quantum complexity, and quantum cryptography. I also have broader interests in classical pseudorandomness and derandomization.
